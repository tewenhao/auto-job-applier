# Worked example (synthetic)

One fully worked entry, showing how raw input sorts into the four blocks. The
candidate and details here are **invented for illustration** — not a real
person. Study how each sentence lands in the right block, and how gaps and
unverified numbers are handled honestly rather than smoothed over.

## Raw input the candidate gave

> did a summer internship at a logistics startup called Cartway. built their
> internal tool for routing delivery vans. was python + a bit of react. i think
> it cut planning time a lot but honestly i never measured it properly. i was
> the only intern, worked directly with the CTO. really liked that it was small
> enough that i could see my stuff get used the next day. don't oversell this —
> it was a prototype, not sure it's still in use after i left. repo is on the
> company's private github so no link.

## Refined entry

```markdown
### Software Engineering Intern — Cartway (logistics startup), Remote. June 2025 - August 2025

*Sole intern; worked directly with the CTO.*

**FACTS:**
- Built an internal **route-planning tool** for the delivery-van fleet: given
  the day's drops, it proposes van routes for the dispatch team.
- **Stack**: Python (routing/backend), a small **React** front-end for dispatchers.
- **My role**: sole developer of the tool, working directly with the CTO on a
  small team — I could ship a change and see dispatch use it the next day.
- **Status**: shipped as an internal **prototype** during the internship;
  whether it remained in use after I left is unknown.

**VOICE:**
- The part I valued was the tightness of the loop: a company small enough that
  something I built was in real use the next day, not queued behind six teams.
  That immediacy is what I look for.

**PRIVATE — do not surface externally:**
- I believe it cut route-planning time substantially but **never measured it** —
  do NOT state a time-saved or efficiency figure. If scale is needed, frame it
  honestly as "a prototype used by the dispatch team during the internship."
- Don't imply it's still in production — adoption after I left is unknown.

**LINKS:**
- No public repo — internal to the company's private GitHub.
```

## Why each move was made

- **"cut planning time a lot but never measured it"** → the *claim* is dropped
  from FACTS (unsourced metric = liability) and preserved honestly as a PRIVATE
  constraint. No number is invented.
- **"could see my stuff get used the next day"** → a genuine reflection in the
  candidate's own voice → VOICE, lightly tidied but keeping their point and tone.
- **"don't oversell / not sure it's still in use"** → guidance, not fact →
  PRIVATE, and the FACTS status line is corrected to `prototype` / `unknown`.
- **"only intern, worked with the CTO"** → framing context → the italic line
  under the heading, and echoed precisely in FACTS as the role.
- **No link** → stated plainly in LINKS rather than omitted, so the absence is
  intentional and clear.
